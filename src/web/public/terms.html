3. Third-party data, and its limits

Market data comes from DexScreener. Risk scores come from RugCheck (Solana) and GoPlus Security (EVM). New-token feeds come from DexScreener and PumpPortal. The operator does not control these services and does not verify their output.

Understand specifically that:

  • A risk score is a heuristic, not a guarantee. Tokens rated low risk are still routinely rugged.
  • An absence of warnings means no warnings were returned, not that a token is safe.
  • Token tickers are not unique. Scammers deliberately clone the names of well-known tokens. Where the bot matched something by ticker rather than by contract address, it says so and shows the address — always check the contract address before acting.
  • Prices can be stale, wrong, or drawn from a manipulated pool.
  • Third-party services can be down, rate-limited, or return errors, in which case features silently do less.
